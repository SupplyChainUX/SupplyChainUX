About Me

Hi, I'm Oscar.

As a dual citizen of Ecuador and the U.S., I've always been fascinated by how goods travel across borders. That curiosity led me to study Supply Chain Management at the University of Nebraska at Kearney — and eventually to partnering with leading supply chain companies to solve real operational problems.

I specialize in supply chain and logistics software because I understand the domain deeply enough to navigate the messy complexity of global supply chains. I don't design from assumptions — I work from lived operational reality.
